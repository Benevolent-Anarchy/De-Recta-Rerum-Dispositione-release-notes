# De Recta Rerum Dispositione 0.2.0

<!-- sourceCommit: 35f8290fa63b5096300bb1f744f0775af75698a4; previousCommit: c22b99e0febe718130344f67b6dd1c706766423c -->

The monastery has sound, and its rooms are larger.

## New

- **Music on the main menu**: Kevin MacLeod's "Gregorian Chant". It loops without a break and fades out as the game opens.
- **Sound effects** throughout. In the application: windows opening, closing and docking, dialogs, menus, items chosen, and a greyed-out item refused. For controls: picked up, put home, attached, laid loose or going back, and toggles, sliders and scrollbars. In the rooms: the opening's fall and a quiet room tone; things picked up and set down, falling, growing and shrinking; the chapel's door, its chains and switch; books and the Abbot's notes; and the servant's pocket, footsteps on wood and on stone, hops, landings and the knock as they tap Open…. Something carried is silent between being picked up and set down.
- **Sounds come from where they happen**, left to right across the whole screen. The servant's footsteps move with them, a window sounds where it opens, a right-click menu where it opens, and a dragged control at the pointer. The music, the room tone and a page sliding on the main menu stay in the middle.
- **Settings… → Audio** holds Volume and Mute, the same ones as the in-game Options dialog, a volume each for Music, Effects and Voice, and **Mono**, which plays every sound alike in both ears. The servant has no voice yet, so Voice has nothing to play.
- **An outline under the pointer.** Rest the pointer on something in the servant's room that a click would act on, and it is outlined, half as thick as a selection. It doesn't show on a control lying in the room, in a room the servant isn't in, or while you drag a control.

## Changed

- **Every room is drawn half as large again**, so its window can grow half as wide and half as tall again. If the rooms are too large for your screen, zoom the workspace out with Ctrl+−. At 1920 × 1080, the cell and the chapel side by side need a notch of zoom out; at 1280 × 720, the chapel opens narrowed, with a scrollbar.
- **Settings… shows its sections as tabs** across the top: Display, Accessibility, Audio and Controls. Click one, or press Up from a tab's first row and then Left or Right.

## Saves

Campaigns from 0.1.0 continue, and your settings carry over.
