# De Recta Rerum Dispositione 0.1.0

<!-- sourceCommit: c22b99e0febe718130344f67b6dd1c706766423c; previousCommit: b451277aa3fd948155ccb7d64d9407f2ded80073 -->

Each room now shows what it expects of the servant.

## New

- **Each room has a duty.** In the cell, Open… goes back in the File menu, and then the servant finds the way on. In the chapel, the dark lamp is lit, and then the servant goes on through the door. In the library, the chained book is read.
- **The servant notices it.** Standing free in a room, they turn towards what it needs next and think of it, once for each thing it needs.
- **A note from the Abbot** hangs on the back wall of each room. Read it as you read the lectern's book. Its wording is still a draft.
- **The servant hints when play stalls.** If what a room needs waits undone for a while, the servant walks over to it, looks at it, points, and turns to you. The wait is never shown. It pauses while a menu, a dialog or the end card is open, while anything is carried, and while the servant's room is out of sight.
- **File → Close (Ctrl+W)** closes the window in front, as its × does. Ctrl+F4 still closes it too. Drop Close on an open door and the servant goes and shuts it.

## Changed

- **The Abbot's notes are handwritten**, in Caveat, on paler paper with a red pin, so they read apart from a book. A note too long for its page scrolls with the mouse wheel, the Up and Down keys, and Page Up and Page Down, with no scrollbar needed.
- **Room windows snap together only where their doorways could make a passage.** The library no longer snaps to the cell.
- **Reset UI also sends a window's own scrollbars home**, from any room or from the servant's pocket, while that window is open.
- **An open door can be reached through its empty doorway.** Drop a control there to attach it to the door, right-click there for the door's menu, or click there to select the door and send the servant to it.
- **A scrollbar dropped on a door lies on the door's face**, and stays within reach of the keyboard.

## Fixed

- A note's text is no longer drawn a size or more smaller than it needs to be, and a note no longer runs past its page when its window shrinks while you read it. The smallest room windows now leave room for a page's text.
- The page keys scroll only a page in the window in front, and not while a menu or dialog has the keyboard.
- A key you gave to another action stays yours: Close does not take Ctrl+W from it.
- A control picked up out of a menu, a dialog or Options stays out of its slot, with its shortcut off, until you let it go.

## Saves

Campaigns from 0.0.5 can't be continued, because the monastery has changed. Continue and Load Game say so. Start a New Game.
